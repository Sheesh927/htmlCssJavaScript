# Carson Intake/Questining Form
## part 1: Research

# Business Goals and Purpose
- What is the primary purpose of the website (e.g., lead generation, direct e-commerce sales, or brand awareness)? 
- How do you measure the success of this website (e.g., number of form submissions, online orders, or phone calls)?
- Who are your main competitors, and what do you like or dislike about their current websites?
- Do you have a portfolio of websites you’ve designed and developed for other small businesses like mine that you can show me?
# Target Audience
- Who is your ideal customer or target demographic?
- What specific action do you want a visitor to take immediately upon landing on the page?
# Content and Branding
- Do you have existing branding guidelines, logos, and color palettes, or do those need to be created?
- Who is responsible for writing the copy, providing high-resolution imagery, and supplying product data?
# Functional Requirements
- Are there any brand materials already in existence that we need to match or coordinate with? Any colors, typefaces we must use? Do you have any requirements for your visual brand design?
- What specific features do you need (e.g., contact forms, booking calendars, user login portals, or e-commerce carts)?
- Are there any third-party tools or software (like a CRM or email marketing platform) that the site must integrate with?
# Maintenance and Logistics
- What is your target launch date and overall budget for the project?
- Who will handle ongoing updates, hosting renewals, and security backups after the site goes live?
- How do you handle website security for the small business websites that you develop and maintain?
- How many other projects are you currently working on?
- What happens after completion of the website?
# Contacting
- What are your typical project timelines and what factors might affect them?
