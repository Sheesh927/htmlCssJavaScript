The story starts out where a normal Salvager in a world above the clouds. His job makes the bare minimum of money as he dives down into the clouds with a harness on and collects loot he finds below the clouds. He pulls back up and finds chests, scrap, and other various items which make him his salary. One day a "normal" person named Malos comes and finds Rex the Salvager and asks Rex to come with him and so Rex listens and they find themselves in an under the cloud sea type base called The Ancient Ship. Then they arrive at The Sealed Room thats deeply hidden at the bottom of the ship secured by an ether-powered gate only Rex can open for some odd reason.

Now skipping forward he awakens a powerblade and in the game you have to go fight various monsters and blades to prgress through the game as malos is evil and killed Rex, But!! Rex came back to life because of the aegies who sacrifieced half of her life force to resurect Rex so he can save the world from Malos and Amalthus(which is Malos driver)
