Lesson 04: Example Promo Scripts

Two examples of scripts are here, plus a blank template. Do not copy these, your script has to be about your own site.

Remember the limits: about 45 words, two lego characters max, say the site name at the end, and it has to time out at 17 seconds or under when you read it out loud.

Example 1: Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Central (a fan site)

41 words. Two characters: REX and PYRA.

Seconds Who Line What's on screen
0–4 REX Where do we find rare Core Crystals in Alrest? Rex figure holding a sword, looking lost next to a Lego cloud-sea build
4–8 PYRA Check Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Central. It has full Blade guides, salvage maps, and affinity charts. Pyra figure steps in, holding a paper "map" tile
8–12 REX Even the best Core Crystal farming spots? Rex turns to Pyra, raising an arm in excitement
12–15 PYRA Everything you need for your journey. Pyra points toward the map tile
15–17 REX Search Xenoblade Chronicles 2 Central for core crystals today Both figures stand side by side, site name in big text across the screen

Shot list for Example 1

One line per picture setup. You'll shoot 40 to 60 pictures total, so each setup below is a small handful of pictures with tiny movements between them.

  1. Rex beside the cloud-sea build, turning head left and right
  2. Pyra enters from the right, walking small steps holding the map tile
  3. Rex turns to face Pyra and raises his right arm
  4. Close-up shot of Pyra pointing at the paper map tile
  5. Rex and Pyra both turn to face forward, posing side by side
  6. Both figures stay still, holding for the site name overlay
