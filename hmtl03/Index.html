Storyline of Xenoblade Chronicles 2

About Contact

The story starts out where a normal Salvager in a world above the clouds. His job makes the bare minimum of money as he dives down into the clouds with a harness on and collects loot he finds below the clouds. He pulls back up and finds chests, scrap, and other various items which make him his salary. One day a "normal" person named Malos comes and finds Rex the Salvager and asks Rex to come with him and so Rex listens and they find themselves in an underground type base.
